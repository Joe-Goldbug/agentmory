# 2026-06-07: Enforce truth during planning and execution

- In Codex work, the anti-lying and anti-wrong-action rule applies during planning and execution, not only after problems are encountered.
- Before planning or executing a step, verify the premise, target state, and success criteria are correct.
- If the planned action is based on a wrong assumption or will lead to a wrong result, stop and correct the plan before execution.
- Do not let an incorrect plan advance into execution just because it seems plausible.
